Hi,

Thank you for testing nodo. Your feedback shaped this version: it is simpler, it starts from the files you work on, and it tells you who matters on each one. Here is what changed.

01

Today shows what moved

What changed on your files since your last look, the next 7 days day by day, and the people on the files that move this week.

02

Calendar is the agenda of your files

Every working party, committee vote and plenary step on the files you follow, week by week. The Parliament calendar is one tap away.

03

17 files, grouped by policy area

Digital, industry, energy, health, budget. Each file opens on its rapporteurs, shadows, Commissioners, cabinet staff and Council working party, with the latest developments.

04

Search speaks Brussels

Type “omnibus”, “pharma package” or “SEP”. For a file nodo doesn’t follow yet, generate its page in one tap.

05

Profiles you can act on

Email an MEP, save them or add them to a file. Each file’s list of people is yours to edit.

Get the new version

Open nodo from your Home Screen. If you still see the old screens, close the app fully and open it again. For a one-minute walk around, open your profile and tap Take the tour.
